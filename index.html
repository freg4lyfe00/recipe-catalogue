Recipe Cleaner

Overlays any recipe page with just the ingredients and steps — serving-size adjuster and weight conversion included.

Browser Extension Recommended

Works on every site — bypasses ad-blocker detectors and security restrictions that break bookmarklets.

  1. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome
  2. Enable Developer mode (toggle, top-right)
  3. Click Load unpacked → select this folder:
    /Users/naonao/recipe-cleaner
  4. A new icon appears in your toolbar — click it on any recipe page
Bookmarklet — may be blocked by some sites

Drag this to your bookmarks bar, then click it while on a recipe page.
